Do I need an account?

No. Type your name to start a room, or to join one from a link, and you play as a guest. The app signs you in anonymously behind the scenes, so your browser remembers your seat in the room. Clearing your browser data, or switching browser, starts you as a new guest.

Free accounts may be offered later, for things like saved history and permanent rooms. Some of those future features may need an account. Starting and joining rooms as a guest will keep working without one.

Your rooms and data

Rooms are stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Anyone with a room’s link or code can join it and see the names and stories in it. Each vote stays hidden from others until the cards are flipped. Don’t put confidential or personal information in room names, names or stories.
